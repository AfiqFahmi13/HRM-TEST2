Interactive Chapter Summaries & Key Terms

Chapter 4: Employee Selection & Placement

  • Person-Job Fit (P-J Fit): Matching individual KSAOs (Knowledge, Skills, Abilities, Other characteristics) to job requirements[cite: 39].
  • Applicant Tracking System (ATS): Software automating job postings, keyword resume screening, and tracking candidate pipelines[cite: 46].
  • Employment Tests: Includes Cognitive, Physical, Work Samples (e.g., IKEA assembly test), Personality inventories (Big-5, OPQ32r, Unilever Portrait test)[cite: 50, 52, 53, 55, 56, 58].
  • Background Checks: Verifying identity, education, criminal records, and employment history (high APAC discrepancies found in employment & education)[cite: 64].

Chapter 6: Training Employees

  • Training Needs Analysis (TNA): Assessed at 3 levels: Organisation (strategic goals), Job/Task (required skills), and Person (who needs development)[cite: 10].
  • Readiness for Training: Combination of employee characteristics (ability/motivation) and work environment support[cite: 15].
  • Training Methods: Classroom, Audiovisual, Computer-based, On-the-Job Training (OJT), Simulations, VR, and Business Games/Case Studies[cite: 18].
  • Evaluation Measures: Trainee satisfaction, new knowledge/skills, transfer of training, performance improvements, and Return on Investment (ROI)[cite: 29].

Chapter 7: Managing Employee Performance

  • Performance Management (PM): Continuous process of setting expectations, reviewing progress, and supporting contribution[cite: 70]. Purpose is Strategic, Administrative, and Developmental[cite: 74].
  • Appraisal Sources: Manager, Peers, Subordinates, Self-appraisal, Customers, and 360-Degree Feedback[cite: 77, 78].
  • Measurement Methods: Graphic Rating Scales, BARS (Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scales), Productivity/Results measures, and Forced-distribution (normal curve)[cite: 78, 83].
  • Rater Errors: Halo/Horn effects, Leniency/Strictness, Central Tendency, Recency error, and Similar-to-me bias[cite: 85, 87, 88].
