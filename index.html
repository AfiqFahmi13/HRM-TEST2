Lecture Notes & Summaries

Chapter 4: Employee Selection & Placement

  • Person-Job (P-J) Fit & Person-Organization (P-O) Fit: Matching KSAOs with job requirements and aligning individual values with company culture[cite: 131].
  • Applicant Tracking System (ATS): Software automating job postings, candidate screening, tracking, and manager collaboration[cite: 138].
  • Employment Tests: Includes cognitive ability, physical ability, work samples (e.g., IKEA furniture assembly test), and personality inventories (Big-5 and OPQ32r)[cite: 141, 144, 147, 148].
  • Background Checks: Verifying identity, education, employment history, and criminal records[cite: 155].

Chapter 6: Training Employees

  • Training Needs Analysis (TNA): Assessed at 3 levels: Organisation analysis, Job/task analysis, and Person analysis[cite: 101].
  • Training Methods: Classroom instruction, audiovisual, computer-based digital learning, on-the-job training (OJT), simulations, virtual reality (VR), and business games/case studies[cite: 109].
  • Measures of Success: Evaluated via trainee satisfaction, transfer of training, new knowledge/skills, performance improvements, and ROI[cite: 121].

Chapter 7: Managing Employees' Performance

  • Purposes: Strategic alignment, administrative decisions (pay/bonuses), and developmental feedback[cite: 166].
  • Appraisal Sources: Manager, peers, subordinates, self-appraisal, customer appraisal, and 360-degree feedback[cite: 167, 169].
  • Rater Errors: Similar-to-me, contrast, leniency/strictness, central tendency, recency, favoritism, and halo/horn effects[cite: 176].
