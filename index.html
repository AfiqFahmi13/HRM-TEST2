Comprehensive Slide Summaries

Chapter 4: Selecting Employees & Placing Them in Jobs[cite: 35]

  • Fit Types: Person-Job fit (KSAOs vs job requirements) and Person-Organization fit (values, culture, goals)[cite: 39].
  • Selection Process Steps: Application/Resume review ➔ Preliminary interview ➔ Selection tests ➔ Employment interviews ➔ Background & reference checks ➔ Selection decision ➔ Physical examination[cite: 41].
  • ATS (Applicant Tracking System): Software managing job postings, automated AI screening, tracking, and manager access[cite: 46].
  • Employment Tests: Cognitive ability tests, Achievement/Aptitude tests, Physical ability tests (strength, dexterity, balance), Work sample tests (e.g., IKEA furniture assembly), and Personality inventories (Big-5: Openness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Agreeableness, Emotional Stability; OPQ32r)[cite: 50, 52, 53, 55, 56].
  • Interview Types: Nondirective, Structured, Situational, and Behavior Description Interview (BDI). Formats include One-on-one, Group, and Board/Panel interviews[cite: 61, 62].
  • Background Checks: Verifying identity, education, employment history, criminal records, and credit checks (APAC stats: high discrepancies found in employment verification)[cite: 64].

Chapter 6: Training Employees[cite: 2]

  • Benefits: Stronger performance, confidence, retention (No. 1 retention strategy per LinkedIn 2025 report)[cite: 5].
  • Training Design Stages: 1. Assess needs ➔ 2. Ensure readiness ➔ 3. Plan programme ➔ 4. Implement training ➔ 5. Evaluate results[cite: 9].
  • Training Needs Analysis (TNA): Assessed at 3 levels: Organisation analysis, Job/task analysis, and Person analysis[cite: 10].
  • Readiness for Training: Employee characteristics (ability, motivation) + Work environment support (time, budget, manager support)[cite: 15].
  • Training Methods: Classroom instruction, Audiovisual, Computer-based/Digital learning, On-the-job training (OJT), Simulations, Virtual Reality (VR), and Business Games/Case Studies[cite: 18].
  • Measures of Success: Trainee satisfaction, Transfer of training, New knowledge & skills, Performance improvements, and Return on Investment (ROI)[cite: 29].

Chapter 7: Managing Employees' Performance[cite: 68]

  • Purposes of PM: Strategic (align goals), Administrative (rewards/bonuses/salary), and Developmental (identify strengths/weaknesses and training)[cite: 74].
  • Appraisal Sources: Manager/Supervisor, Peers, Subordinates, Self-appraisal, Customer appraisal, and 360-degree multi-rater feedback[cite: 77, 78].
  • Measurement Methods: Graphic Rating Scales, Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scales (BARS), Productivity/Results measures (quantitative targets), and Forced-distribution (normal bell curve)[cite: 79].
  • Common Rater Errors: Similar-to-me, Contrast error, Leniency/Strictness, Central tendency, Recency error, Favoritism, and Halo/Horn effect[cite: 85].
  • Feedback Approaches: Tell-and-sell, Tell-and-listen, Problem-solving, Coaching & development, and Continuous feedback check-ins[cite: 90].
